# Storyboard — "Smaller than the stadium"

**Piece**: LOCK #1a, Piece 2 — Curaçao vs Germany ranking-gap reveal
**Audience hook**: David vs Goliath story for the smallest nation ever in a men's WC
**Length**: 14 seconds
**Resolution**: 1080×1920 (9:16 vertical)
**Posting**: IG Reels + TikTok + YT Shorts on launch day

---

## The premise (what the viewer learns in 14 seconds)

Curaçao — a 150,000-person Caribbean island — qualified for the 2026 World Cup. They are the smallest nation EVER to qualify. Their first match is against Germany, ranked #11 in the world. The gap is unprecedented in WC history.

**The single moment the viewer remembers**: "Smaller than the stadium."

## Reference points (what we're borrowing from)

- **ESPN's** "Did You Know?" stat-reveal format (single-shot, big number, hold for impact)
- **@433's** punch-line cadence (single statistic per video, no list)
- **@statmusefb's** big-number reveals on Instagram (Anton font, full-screen text)
- **NYT Upshot's** "fact stack" graphics (one stat = one screen, type-driven)
- **The Pudding's** scrollers (each beat a separate visual state, no continuous animation)

What we are NOT copying:
- ❌ A 48-row bar chart (the previous garbage render)
- ❌ Multiple stats at once
- ❌ Continuous physics motion (this is a stat-reveal, not a marble race)

## The shot list (frame by frame)

### SHOT 1 — Hook (0:00 → 0:02) · 60 frames

**Visual**:
- Solid pitch-green flash (1 frame), then dark-gradient background
- Centered: massive Anton ALL CAPS text "WHAT IF"
- Below it, smaller Inter Bold: "the smallest country ever"
- Below that, "qualified for the World Cup?"
- Subtle pitch.predict watermark top-left

**Camera move**: zoom slowly in (1.05× over 2s) — adds tension

**Audio**: ambient pad starts here, a single soft "tick" at the question mark

**Text on screen**:
- "WHAT IF" (Anton, 140px, ALL CAPS)
- "the smallest country ever" (Inter Bold, 56px)
- "qualified for the World Cup?" (Inter Bold, 56px)

### SHOT 3 — Population context (0:04 → 0:07) · 90 frames

**Visual**:
- Animated dot grid: 150,000 dots filling the bottom 60% of the screen
- They flood in over 1s (fast wash from edges), then HOLD
- Anton ALL CAPS overlay top: "150,000 PEOPLE"
- Sub-label in Inter Bold: "smaller than MetLife Stadium can hold"
- Below: a single empty stadium icon outline (MetLife = 82,500 seats; visualize the gap: 150K total nation vs ~83K stadium = nation barely 2× a single venue)

**Camera move**: dots flood, then SLOW slow-zoom out (1.0 → 0.94) so the viewer perceives "all of this... is just this small"

**Audio**: dot-rain sound effect (high frequencies sparkle), then a single deep bass note on "smaller than" reveal

### SHOT 4 — The opponent (0:07 → 0:09) · 60 frames

**Visual**:
- Quick cut. Black background.
- Massive Anton "GERMANY" in white
- Below: "#11 in the world"
- German flag (flat block — black/red/yellow) bottom of frame
- Subtitle: "First match. Day one."

**Camera move**: hold, then slight push-in on "First match. Day one."

**Audio**: stadium roar swell at 0:07, ducks under voice on 0:08

---

### SHOT 5 — The gap (0:09 → 0:12) · 90 frames

**Visual** (the money shot):
- Split screen: LEFT half = Curaçao (blue tones), RIGHT half = Germany (black/red)
- Both sides show their Elo rating animated counting up: CUR: 0 → 1436, GER: 0 → 1923
- Counters animate at the same speed for ~1.5s — both climbing
- Curaçao stops at 1436. Germany keeps going. Long pause.
- Germany finally lands at 1923.
- A 487-pixel-wide visualization of the GAP between them appears, with the number "487" in giant Anton red.
- Below: "the biggest gap in World Cup history"

**Camera move**: 
- During the count-up: slight shake on each tick (broadcast-feel)
- When CUR stops + GER continues: brief slow-mo on GER's continuing climb
- On "the biggest gap": pull back to show both halves at once

**Audio**: 
- Count-up: a rising arpeggio synced to the numbers
- CUR stops: a sharp "thunk"
- GER continues: the arpeggio keeps rising solo
- On "the biggest gap": beat drops

---

### SHOT 6 — Payoff (0:12 → 0:14) · 60 frames

**Visual**:
- Cut to black. Hold for 6 frames (gives the viewer's brain a beat).
- Fade in: Anton ALL CAPS giant white text: "SMALLER THAN THE STADIUM"
- Sub-line in Inter Semibold: "going to the World Cup"
- Bottom CTA band: "BACK CURAÇAO. THEY'RE BACK CURAÇAO. JUNE 14."
- Brand watermark visible

**Camera move**: hold static. No motion. Let the line breathe.

**Audio**: beat drops, then dies into silence on the last 1s. Final reverb tail.

## What success looks like

A viewer scrolling at 7pm:
1. Stops on "WHAT IF" (visual contrast + curiosity gap)
2. Reads "smallest country ever qualified" (cognitive engagement)
3. Watches population dots flood (visual satisfaction)
4. Sees the Elo count-up (tension build)
5. Sees the 487-point gap reveal (payoff)
6. Comments: "RESPECT CURAÇAO" / "this is wild" / "underdog of the century" / "going to germany away first match wtf"
7. Shares with friends who are football fans

Target metrics for this specific piece:
- ≥40% retention at 5s
- ≥60% completion (viewers who watch to 13s)
- ≥0.8% comment-to-view ratio (this should outperform our floor because the story is so loaded)
- ≥3 saves per 100 views (people will save to share later)
- ≥1.5 shares per 100 views (the underdog story is share-bait)

## What we're NOT doing (and why)

- ❌ A 14-second dot-by-dot 150,000 dot animation — too slow visually, won't read on small screens. We flood them in 1s, then HOLD.
- ❌ Showing all 48 teams — the previous mistake. ONE comparison only.
- ❌ Music with vocals — language barrier and IP risk. Instrumental only.
- ❌ Player faces — IP risk + we don't have rights. Flags and country names only.
- ❌ "Bet on Curaçao" framing — platform-risky. Use "back Curaçao" / "root for".
- ❌ A linear "explain the format" piece — this is an emotional underdog reveal, not an explainer.

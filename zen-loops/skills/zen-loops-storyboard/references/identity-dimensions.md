# Identity dimensions for zen-loops

The viral multiplier in this brand is identity projection. Pick wisely.

## Tier 1 — Universal, evergreen

| Dimension | Marbles | When it spikes | Audience reach |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Birth month** | 12 (JAN–DEC) | Evergreen baseline | Universal |
| **Zodiac sign** | 12 (ARI–PIS) | New year, Mercury retrograde, eclipse weeks | Astrology Twitter, TikTok girls |
| **Initial / first letter** | 26 (A–Z) | Evergreen; needs smaller marbles + more rows | Universal |
| **Birth decade** | 6 (60s–10s) | Nostalgia content waves | Wide age range |

## Tier 2 — Cultural / topical

| Dimension | Marbles | When it spikes | Audience |

## Picking the right dimension

Match the audience you're trying to grow into:

- **Growing TikTok casual**: birth month, zodiac, initial → maximum projection rate
- **Football vertical**: country, league teams
- **Finance vertical**: tickers, crypto coins
- **Astrology vertical**: zodiac, birth chart elements

## Anti-patterns

- **More than 26 marbles** → eye can't track on mobile; cap at 26 max
- **Identity dimensions requiring specific knowledge** (e.g., chess opening ELO range, niche cooking technique) → too small audience
- **Mixing identity dimensions in one piece** ("your zodiac AND your birth year") → cognitive load too high
- **Identity dimension that's offensive or excludes large groups** (religion-coded for diverse audience, etc.)

## Storyboarding tip

For each piece, the storyboard should specify ONE identity dimension. If the user proposes two, ask which one to lock for THIS piece — the other can be a follow-up post.
